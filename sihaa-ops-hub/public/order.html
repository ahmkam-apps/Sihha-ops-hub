How Food Assistance Works

Food orders are no longer placed online. Everything is handled automatically through WhatsApp once your family is enrolled.

  1. Register your household using the form below — takes about 2 minutes.
  2. A coordinator reviews your request and approves your enrollment, usually within 48 hours.
  3. Before each delivery cycle, you'll receive a WhatsApp message asking you to confirm or skip that delivery.
  4. Reply to confirm and your bundle will be prepared and delivered by a volunteer on the scheduled date.

Deliveries run bi-weekly on Saturdays and Sundays. No app or account required — just WhatsApp.
